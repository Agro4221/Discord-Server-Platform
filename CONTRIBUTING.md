# Development Rules

- Keep secrets out of Git.
- Prefer cohesive modules over cross-module coupling.
- A reproducible bug gets regression coverage.
- Avoid Administrator permission requirements without an explicit reason.
- Isolate external providers behind adapters.
- Persistent state must be recoverable after restart.
- Update docs/WORK-LOG.md and docs/PROJECT-STATE.md after substantial work.
- Update docs/TEST-MATRIX.md for new subsystems.
